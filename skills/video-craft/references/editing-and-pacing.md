# Editing and pacing

## Rhythm
- Short video (60 to 120s): a visual change every 3 to 6 seconds; a new idea every 8 to 12.
- Alternate: action scene, then a 1s breathing hold, then the next action.
- Enter late, leave early: start the scene on the first action, cut after the result registers.

## What to cut
- Page loads, spinners longer than 1s, SMS waits, typing of long strings (speed 2 to 2.5x, never skip).
- Repeats: the second time a pattern appears, shorten it.

## What to keep
- Result frames (3s).
- The one failure and its recovery.
- The support contact (hold 4s with a tooltip or card).

## Structure and length
| Type | Length | Scenes |
|------|--------|--------|
| Feed promo | 20 to 45s | 3 to 5 |
| Single task tutorial | 60 to 120s | 6 to 10 |
| Series episode | 2 to 4 min | 8 to 14 |

## Hand-off beats
Intro card 3s, hold the result 3s, outro card 3s. Nothing else is padding.

## Review loop
1. Watch on mute: does the picture alone make sense?
2. Watch with audio only: does the voice alone make sense?
3. Watch both at 1.25x: does anything feel slow?
