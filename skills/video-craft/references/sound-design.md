# Sound design

- **Voice first.** Narration at -15 LUFS integrated, true peak below -1.2 dBTP.
- **Cleanup:** high-pass at 70 Hz, light compression (ratio 2.5:1), no gating that chops breaths.
- **Silence is a tool.** Keep 0.3s of room tone between scene changes rather than dead air.
- **Music (optional):** a quiet instrumental bed at -28 to -24 LUFS, ducked 6 to 9 dB under voice with a 0.3s attack and 0.6s release. No lyrics. No music that competes with Bangla speech.
- **Effects (optional):** a soft click on button press, a keyboard tick on typing, at -30 LUFS. Never on every action.
- **Edges:** 20 ms fades on every cut to avoid clicks.
- **Delivery:** AAC 192 kbps, 48 kHz stereo. Check on phone speakers and on headphones.
- **Rights:** only use music and effects with a licence that allows commercial use; keep the licence note in the project folder.
