# Voice direction

## Brief to the voice (TTS or human)
- Persona: a patient teacher helping a neighbour, not an announcer.
- Pace: medium-slow, about 2.2 words per second.
- Energy: warm, light smile; lift slightly on the promise and the result, calm on warnings.
- Pauses: comma 0.25s, sentence 0.5s, scene change 0.8s.

## Recording lines
- One file for the whole script is the default: paragraphs separated by 2 s of silence, so the pieces can be cut exactly. Per-line files are the fallback.
- Mono or stereo, 44.1 or 48 kHz, no music, no reverb.
- Leave 0.2s of silence at the head and 0.3s at the tail of each file.
- Same room, same mic distance, same session for a whole video; re-record whole lines, not words.
- Keep a take log: line, take number, chosen.

## TTS tips
- Use the style prompt in the speaker-line sheet (warm, calm teacher, natural pauses).
- Generate per line, listen, regenerate only the lines that sound flat.
- Spell hard words phonetically in a glossary; check numbers, brand names and abbreviations.
- Do not mix voices inside a video.

## Direction vocabulary
| Note | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| lift | slightly higher pitch and pace |
| land | slow, firm ending |
| soft | lower volume, reassuring |
| beat | half-second pause before the next idea |

## Review
Listen at normal speed on phone speakers. If a line needs a second listen to understand, simplify it.
