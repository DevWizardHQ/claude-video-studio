# Scriptwriting

## Write for the ear
- Sentences of 6 to 14 words. One idea each.
- Put the verb early. Put the thing to click last: "ওপরের নিবন্ধন বাটনে ক্লিক করুন।"
- Use commas where a breath belongs; the voice pauses there, and so does the sync.
- Prefer concrete nouns and the exact label on screen. Quote the button as it reads.
- Numbers as spoken ("ছয় অঙ্কের কোড"), abbreviations as pronounced ("ওটিপি").

## Structure of a line
`[where to look] + [what to do] + [what will happen]`, but shorten to what the viewer needs now.
Example: "এবার নিচের ঘরে ফোন নম্বর দিন। এই নম্বরেই কোড যাবে।"

## Timing budget
- Speech rate: about 2.2 to 2.6 words per second for Bangla tutorials; slower than conversation.
- A typing action needs the line to start as typing starts, not after.
- A click line should end at or just after the click.
- A scene is 4 to 9 seconds. Anything longer needs a visual change.

## Style
- Address the viewer as "আপনি". Warm, not formal, not slangy.
- Avoid "simply", "just", "easy"; they sound dismissive when the viewer is stuck.
- Do not read the screen aloud. Say what matters on it.
- Do not repeat the caption word for word if captions are burned in; shorten the caption.

## Edge-case lines
State the cause, the action, the fallback, and the limit: "কোড না এলে ৬০ সেকেন্ড অপেক্ষা করুন, তারপর আবার পাঠান বাটনে ক্লিক করুন। বারবার না এলে সাপোর্টে যোগাযোগ করুন।"

## Hand-off format
Speaker lines `L01...`, one idea each, delivery note per line, pronunciation glossary, no lines over two short sentences.
