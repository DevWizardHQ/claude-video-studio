# Color and brand

- Take colors, logo and font from the product, not from taste. Record them once in `brand.json`.
- Stage background: a dark tint of the primary, gradient to the primary, with a slow light drift (opacity 0.1 to 0.15).
- Text on dark: `#fff` or `#f4f7f4`. Secondary text: a tint of the primary at 70% lightness.
- Accent for the cursor and highlights: a warm amber (`#f59e0b`), high contrast against green and white UI.
- Contrast: caption text at least 7:1 against its box; UI tooltips at least 4.5:1.
- Use at most three colors: primary, dark, accent. Everything else is neutral.
- Logo on white pill when placed over the gradient; never stretched, never recolored.
- A series keeps one palette, one intro, one outro, one caption style.
- Avoid pure black and pure white as large fills; they flicker after compression.
