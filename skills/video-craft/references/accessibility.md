# Accessibility

- **Subtitles:** always deliver `.srt` and `.vtt`, even when text is burned in.
- **Contrast:** captions 7:1, tooltips 4.5:1, never color alone to mean something.
- **Reading speed:** at most 15 characters per second; at least 1.2s per caption.
- **Motion:** no flashing above 3 times per second; zoom and pan are slow and eased.
- **Audio:** speech never competes with music; no information carried by sound alone.
- **Language:** plain words, defined abbreviations, short sentences; reading level suited to the audience in the brief.
- **Text size:** body captions never below 28px on a 1920 canvas; legible on a phone.
- **Alternative to video:** provide the step list as text (the approved script table works) next to the video.
- **Sensitive data:** nothing personal on screen, even blurred beyond use; use test data.
