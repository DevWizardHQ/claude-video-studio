## Standing principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** The viewer finishes the task, or the video failed.
2. **One idea per scene, one action per sentence.**
3. **Show, then say, then confirm.** The cursor arrives, the voice names it, the result appears.
4. **Restraint.** Every effect must remove confusion or add emphasis. Otherwise delete it.
5. **Consistency.** Same easing, same durations, same caption position across a series.
6. **Respect the viewer.** No jargon without a gloss, no flashing, no fake urgency, no personal data.
