# Video Story

The story is approved before the script is written. Changing a story costs minutes; changing a recorded video costs hours.

## Produce `story.md` from `references/story-template.md`

1. **Viewer and problem**: who watches and what stops them today.
2. **Promise**: what they can do after the video, one sentence.
3. **Hook** (first 5 seconds): the question, the pain, or the result shown first.
4. **Arc**: setup, steps, a snag, recovery, result, next action.
5. **Scene map**: numbered scenes, one line each, with the screen used and the single idea.
6. **Snags**: the edge cases chosen in the brief, placed where a real viewer would hit them.
7. **Tone and pacing**: register, pace, how many seconds per scene.
8. **Call to action**: the one thing to do next, plus the support contact.

## Present and iterate

Show the whole story, not a summary. Ask:
- Is the order right?
- Is anything missing or too long?
- Is the promise the right promise?

Apply changes, show the diff in the scene map, ask again. Only an explicit approval moves to `video-script-review`.

## Principles

- One video, one job. Split a second job into a second video.
- Start with the result or the problem, never with the logo.
- Show the snag and the fix; trust comes from honesty about failure.
- End on an action the viewer can take in under a minute.
